Gamma Spectroscopy AR

Follow a gamma photon into an HPGe or NaI(Tl) detector, watch it become an electrical pulse, and see where it lands in the energy spectrum. Then build full spectra and compare the two detectors.

Surface AR needs a phone browser with WebXR (Chrome on Android). On iPhone, use the marker or the 3D view.

Need the marker? Print it (the GM Counter sheet works too) or show it on a second screen, at least 8 cm wide.

Animations are slowed down by about a billion times. Real gamma radiation is invisible.

Efficiency is how many photons end up in the peak. Resolution is how narrow the peak is. They are separate properties: NaI(Tl) here is more efficient, HPGe resolves far better.

Detector dimensions (illustrative)

Bigger crystals catch more photons and keep more scattered photons inside, so efficiency and the photopeak fraction rise. Resolution does not change.
