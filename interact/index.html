Radiation–Matter Interactions

Fire electrons, beta particles, protons, alphas, neutrons, gamma rays or x-rays into air, water, polyethylene, aluminium, concrete or lead. Watch every ionization and excitation along the track, then zoom into single atoms to see how each process works.

Surface AR needs Chrome on Android. On iPhone, use the marker or the 3D view.

Need the marker? The same Hiro marker as the other labs. Print it, or show it on a second screen at least 8 cm wide.

Tracks are slowed down by about a billion times. Real radiation is invisible; the dots stand for ionized and excited atoms.
